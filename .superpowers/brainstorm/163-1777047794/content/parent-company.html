How should company accounts work?

When a company like "xTimely Pty Ltd" sends James to sell scrap — how do you want to record it?

A

Company Account with Contact Person

One account for the company. The contact person (James) is just a field inside that account. All transactions go under xTimely Pty Ltd.

Account Name
xTimely Pty Ltd
Contact Person
James Smith
VAT Number
4123456789
Company Reg
2015/012345/07
✓ Simple ✓ Matches your existing schema ⚠ Can't track multiple reps
B

Parent / Child Accounts

Create xTimely Pty Ltd as a parent company account, then James as his own sub-account linked to it. James sells, but transactions are associated with both James and xTimely.

🏢 xTimely Pty Ltd Parent company account
👤 James Smith Rep / contact, linked to xTimely
✓ Multiple reps per company ✓ Company-level reporting ✗ More complex ✗ Schema changes needed
