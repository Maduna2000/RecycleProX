What do Dealer 1 / 2 / 3 mean?

Help me understand how dealer categories actually work in your yard

A

Price Tiers — each dealer level gets different buy prices

Dealer 1 might get the best prices, Dealer 2 slightly lower, Dealer 3 lower still. The category drives what price list they're on.

Product Dealer 1 Dealer 2 Dealer 3
Copper Wire R 85/kg R 80/kg R 75/kg
Steel R 4.50/kg R 4.20/kg R 3.90/kg
B

Size / Volume Tiers — categories reflect how big the supplier is

Dealer 1 = large established scrap yards, Dealer 2 = medium, Dealer 3 = small. Used for reporting and grouping, but pricing is set separately per account via price groups.

Dealer 1 Large established scrap yard
Dealer 2 Medium yard / collector
Dealer 3 Small yard / part-time
Casual Walk-in street seller
C

Both — categories drive price AND describe size

Dealer 1/2/3 is a label that both describes the supplier's size AND is linked to a specific price group. Assigning the category automatically sets their price list.

Dealer 1 → automatically on Price Group A
Dealer 2 → automatically on Price Group B
Dealer 3 → automatically on Price Group C
Casual → default buy prices
