How the filtering works behind the scenes
1 Cashier selects customer → system loads their tradeCommodities (e.g. ["Ferrous","Non-Ferrous"])
2 Product list filters to only show products whose category matches one of those commodities
3 If customer has no commodities set → all products show (no restriction, same as today)
4 Dealer category auto-applies correct price group → prices shown are already correct for that dealer
5 If customer is zero-rated → VAT line does not appear on the purchase slip
