Multi-Product Wizard — Flow Approach

Two ways to structure the "add multiple products" experience in the scale station

A

Cart-Based Flow

Customer → [Product → Weight → Photos] × N → Review → Submit

After each product's photos are captured, the operator sees a running cart of items added so far. They choose "Add Another Product" (loops back to product step) or "Continue to Review". The cart shows a live summary at all times — "2 items added, ready to submit".

✓ Mirrors physical workflow ✓ Can edit/remove items before submit ✓ Clear progress at every step ⚠ More wizard state to manage
B

Linear "Add Another" Flow

Customer → Product → Weight → Photos → [Add Another?] → Review → Submit

After capturing photos for a product, the wizard shows a simple confirmation: "Product added ✓". Two buttons: Add Another Product (jumps to product step, skipping customer) or Done — Go to Review. Minimal UI change from today.

✓ Simplest implementation ✓ Familiar flow to existing operators ⚠ Can't see/edit previous items mid-flow ⚠ No "running total" visibility
My recommendation: A — The cart approach is slightly more work but gives the operator full visibility and control. A yard operator needs to see what's been processed before signing off.
